PAULA'S REVENGE - INSTALL (Mac, Apple Silicon or Intel)   (Windows: see INSTALL-Windows.txt)

Copy Paula's Revenge.app -> /Applications/

Then in Terminal (downloaded files are quarantined by macOS):

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Paula's Revenge.app"

Open it from Applications. Audio and MIDI devices are under
Paula's Revenge > Audio/MIDI Settings... (Cmd+,).

(From v0.8 Paula's Revenge is a standalone app only; the old VST3/AU
plug-ins are no longer built. You can delete them from
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.)

SAMPLE (Cmd+4) records audio: the first time, macOS asks whether
Paula's Revenge may use the microphone / audio input - click OK.
(System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone changes it later.)
