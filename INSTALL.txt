PAULA'S REVENGE - INSTALL (Mac, Apple Silicon or Intel)

Copy Paula's Revenge.app       -> /Applications/
Copy Paula's Revenge.vst3      -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
Copy Paula's Revenge.component -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/

(~ is your home folder. In Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the path.)

Then in Terminal (downloaded files are quarantined by macOS):

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Paula's Revenge.app"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Paula's Revenge.vst3"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Paula's Revenge.component"
  killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar

Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan. It appears as an instrument
under Twisted Genetics.
